01 · Product launch film

NORDA: one launch, three hooks

Spec launch for a fictional sparkling water brand. One body and three hooks to test: an impact shot, a macro detail and a POV trend format. 20 s, 9:16.

Hook B · Impact

"Most water is just wet."

Hook A · Macro detail

"Cold, redefined."

Hook C · POV trend

"POV: the coldest can on earth"

How I made it
1 · ConceptMy idea: sell water like a luxury product. I wrote the script, shot list and three hooks, then used Claude and ChatGPT for research, fact-checking and writing the prompts.
2 · Product referenceOne reference frame of the can made with Nano Banana, used as an ingredient in every shot so the product never changes.
3 · Shots8 shots generated in Google Flow from written prompts: reveal, splash, flavours, lifestyle, end card and three hooks.
4 · EditVoice-over with ElevenLabs, cuts on the beat, captions in the safe area, sound ducked under the voice, colour and exports.
This exact NORDA can opens with a sharp pop, sparkling water erupts upward in super slow motion with crushed ice and thin slices of lime, droplets frozen in the air, black background, cold blue rim light, macro lens, premium drink commercial, fizz sound.
